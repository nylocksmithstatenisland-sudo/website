When a Car Key Is an Emergency

A spare-key appointment can wait for a convenient day. An emergency cannot: the only key is gone, the car is in a lot that closes at 10 PM, a parent is stranded at the St. George ferry terminal, or the key broke off inside the ignition with the groceries in the trunk. Emergency car key replacement means we treat the call like a lockout: the nearest technician is dispatched now, with the key blanks, cutting machine and programming tools in the van, and the job is finished at the car. For planned spares and copies, see our regular car key replacement service.

We cover every situation that leaves you without a working key: all keys lost (we cut from the VIN or by decoding the door lock), key broken in the ignition or door (we extract the fragment and cut a fresh key), stolen keys (we make a new key and erase the stolen ones from the immobilizer so they no longer start the car), and a fob that suddenly stopped working (often a programming or circuit-board fault; see key fob programming). If the key is simply locked inside, you need our faster and cheaper car lockout service instead.

Every technician is a NYC-licensed locksmith Staten Island based on Richmond Avenue, shows a license ID on arrival, and confirms the price you were quoted on the phone before cutting anything.

What Happens When You Call

1

Tell us the car

Year, make, model and where it is. We quote the full price on the phone and give you an arrival window, usually 15-45 minutes.

2

Ownership check

On arrival we check photo ID and the registration or title. This protects you: nobody gets a key to your car without proving it is theirs.

3

Cut and program

The key is cut from the VIN or the lock, then programmed to the immobilizer with dealer-level equipment. Lost or stolen keys are erased on request.

4

Test and drive

We test the doors, ignition and remote functions in front of you. You pay the price you were quoted and drive away, typically 20-45 minutes after we arrive.

What We Can Make at the Car

🔑 Transponder (chip) keys

The standard key on most cars since the late 1990s. Cut and programmed to the immobilizer on site. See transponder key service.

📡 Remote key fobs

Key plus remote in one unit. We supply the fob, cut the emergency blade and program both the chip and the remote buttons.

🚘 Smart / proximity keys

Push-to-start keys for most Japanese, Korean and American makes, and many European ones. Some models need the car's key data read first, which adds time; we say so when you call.

🔧 Laser-cut and older keys

High-security sidewinder blades and plain non-chip keys for older vehicles, cut by code so they work first time.

Stolen Keys: Replace, Then Erase

A stolen key is not just a lost key. Whoever has it can open and start your car until that key is removed from the vehicle's memory. When you tell us the keys were stolen, we make your new key and then erase every other key from the immobilizer, so the stolen one becomes a piece of metal. Bring any remaining keys to the appointment so they can be re-added.

If your house keys were on the same ring, see lost key replacement for the home side of the problem; a rekey is usually the right call when an address was with the keys.

Emergency Car Key Replacement FAQ

I lost every key to my car. Can you still make one?

Yes. We cut a new key from the vehicle's VIN or by decoding the door lock, then program it to the car's immobilizer on site. No tow to a dealer and no waiting days for a key to arrive. Bring photo ID and the registration or title so we can confirm the car is yours.

How long does emergency car key replacement take?

Most jobs are done within 20-45 minutes of arrival: 10-15 minutes to cut the key and 5-30 minutes to program it, depending on the make. A few European models with locked key data can take longer; we tell you on the phone before you book.

Do you replace car keys on weeknights?

Friday, Saturday and Sunday we run 24 hours a day, holidays included. Monday to Thursday we work 9 AM to 6 PM and do not take overnight calls, so a Tuesday-night loss is handled first thing at 9 AM.

Why not just go to the dealer?

The dealer usually needs the car towed in, orders the key, and takes one to three days. We come to the car with the blanks and programming equipment and finish on the spot, and mobile service typically saves an average of $45 against dealer pricing for the same key.

What should I have ready when you arrive?

Photo ID, the registration or title, and if possible the year, make and model when you call. If the keys were stolen, say so: we can erase the lost keys from the immobilizer so they no longer start the car.
