Professional Smart Lock Installation in Staten Island, NY

A smart lock is only as good as the door it sits in. Most Staten Island calls we get about smart locks that "stopped working" come down to a deadbolt that rubs the strike plate, a door that swelled in August, or a retrofit that was mounted on a mortise lock it was never designed for. Our residential locksmith team installs smart and keypad locks on single-family houses, two-family homes, co-ops, condos and rental apartments across the island, and we fix the door before we fit the lock.

Every installation includes alignment of the bolt and strike, a reinforced strike plate where the frame allows it, programming of codes and the app, a mechanical key override that actually works, and a five-minute walkthrough so everyone in the household can use it. If you already own the lock, we install it. If you do not, we bring the model that fits your door and your building's rules.

What moves the price up or down

  • Door prep. A door with a standard deadbolt hole is a straight swap. A door with no deadbolt, an off-size bore or a mortise lock needs drilling or an adapter.
  • Door condition. A sagging door or a strike that does not line up has to be fixed first or the motor will strain and the batteries will die in weeks.
  • Connectivity. Some Bluetooth locks need a plug-in bridge for remote access; Wi-Fi locks need a decent signal at the door, and a mesh node sometimes solves it.
  • Matching hardware. A new handleset or lever to match the lock finish is quoted separately.

Smart Locks in NYC Apartments: Renters, Co-ops and Condos

Most of the questions we get from Staten Island apartment dwellers are about permission, not hardware. Here is how it works in New York City.

Renters: the law is on your side, with one condition

New York Multiple Dwelling Law section 51-c gives tenants in multiple dwellings the right to install their own lock on the apartment entrance door at their own expense, provided it is no more than three inches in circumference and the landlord gets a duplicate key on request. A smart lock that works only by code or app does not satisfy that; one with a key override, or a retrofit that keeps the landlord's cylinder, does. Read your lease as well, since many require written consent for hardware changes.

No-drill options for renters

Retrofit locks (August Wi-Fi Smart Lock, SwitchBot Lock Ultra, Level Bolt) replace only the inside thumb-turn. The outside of the door, the keyway and the landlord's key are untouched, and the lock comes off in ten minutes when you move. This is the install we recommend for most rentals.

Co-ops and condos: ask the board first

House rules often govern the appearance of hallway doors and may require that the super's master key still works. A retrofit lock rarely needs approval; a visible keypad deadbolt usually does. We supply the product spec sheet and a certificate of insurance from Ergo Next for board packages.

Fire code and egress

An apartment door used for exit must open from the inside in one motion without a key. That rules out double-cylinder deadbolts and means every smart lock we install has an inside thumb-turn that works with dead batteries. It also means a landlord cannot require a lock that traps you inside.

Building-wide keyless systems

Some newer buildings run fob or app entry on every apartment door as part of an access-control system. In those buildings management installs and owns the lock, and NYC's Tenant Data Privacy Act limits how the entry data can be used. You can usually still add a retrofit lock inside, subject to the lease.

Landlords and property managers

Keypad locks end the lost-key cycle between tenants: delete the old code, add a new one, no rekey. We install fleets of Schlage, Yale and Kwikset keypad deadbolts for property management accounts with Net-30 billing and per-door pricing.

Apple Home Key, Google Home and Alexa

Schlage Encode Plus, Yale Assure Lock 2, Level Lock+, Kwikset Halo Select and Aqara U100 support Apple Home Key, so an iPhone or Apple Watch tap unlocks the door. Matter-certified locks work with Apple, Google and Amazon at once. Tell us which ecosystem you use and we pick a lock that fits it.

Battery life and dead-battery entry

Expect 6-12 months on Bluetooth and keypad locks and 3-6 months on Wi-Fi. The app warns you for weeks. If the batteries die anyway, most locks take emergency power from a 9-volt battery or USB-C on the outside, and all the ones we install have a physical key cylinder. We hand you that key and show you the terminals at the walkthrough.

Should You Install It Yourself?

Honest answer: a retrofit lock on a door that already closes well is a 20-minute job with a Phillips screwdriver, and the manufacturers' apps walk you through it. Call us when any of the following is true.

Call a locksmith if

  • You have to lift or pull the door to throw the deadbolt now (alignment problem, the motor will fail)
  • The door has a mortise, jimmy-proof or multi-point lock
  • There is no deadbolt and a bore has to be drilled
  • The door is steel, or the bore is off-size from a previous lock
  • You want the strike plate reinforced with 3-inch screws into the stud
  • It is a rental or co-op and you need the landlord's key to keep working
  • You are fitting three or more doors and want the codes managed in one app

What a professional install includes

  • Door and strike alignment so the bolt throws freely with the door closed
  • Reinforced strike plate and correct through-bolts
  • Calibration in the app (the step that fails most DIY installs)
  • User codes for the household, a guest code, and auto-lock timing
  • Key override tested, spare key cut, emergency power terminals shown
  • Old hardware removed and the door left clean
  • Follow-up support for pairing, codes and batteries

Smart Lock Installation FAQ: Staten Island and NYC

The questions we answer most on the phone.

How much does smart lock installation cost in Staten Island and NYC?

Labor is $100-$200 per door, which covers fitting, alignment, app setup, user codes, testing and a walkthrough. Add the lock: $150-$300 for a keypad or Bluetooth deadbolt from Kwikset, Schlage or Yale, $400-$800 for Wi-Fi, fingerprint or touchscreen models. A mortise or jimmy-proof apartment door that needs an adapter or a mortise-specific lock runs $200-$300 labor. NYC-wide guides quote $150-$400 for installation, and we are at the lower end.

Can I put a smart lock on my NYC rental apartment?

In most cases yes. New York Multiple Dwelling Law section 51-c lets a tenant in a multiple dwelling add their own lock to the apartment door at their own expense, as long as it is no more than three inches in circumference and the landlord gets a duplicate key on request. The practical reading: use a retrofit smart lock that keeps the landlord's keyed cylinder, or a smart deadbolt with a key override, and give management a key. Check your lease too; some leases require written consent for any hardware change.

Do I need co-op or condo board approval for a smart lock?

Usually for anything that changes the outside of the door. Co-op house rules often regulate the look of hallway doors, and some buildings require a specific lock that the super can open with a master key. A retrofit lock that mounts only on the inside thumb-turn rarely needs approval; a full keypad deadbolt usually does. We can provide the spec sheet and insurance certificate boards ask for.

Will a smart lock fit a mortise lock or jimmy-proof apartment door?

Yes, with the right product. Older Staten Island houses and many co-op and rental doors use a mortise lock (one long body inside the door edge) or a Segal-style jimmy-proof rim lock. Options include a mortise-specific smart lock, a retrofit that drives the existing thumb-turn, or adding a smart deadbolt above the existing lock when the door allows a new bore. We measure the door prep before quoting.

Can a smart lock be installed without drilling, for renters?

Yes. Retrofit locks such as the August Wi-Fi Smart Lock, SwitchBot Lock and Level Bolt mount over or inside the existing deadbolt, so the outside of the door and your key stay exactly as they were. When you move out, we remove it and the door is untouched.

What happens if the battery dies or the power goes out?

Smart locks run on batteries, not house power, so a blackout changes nothing at the door. When the batteries run down, the lock warns you for weeks in the app and with a beep. If you ignore it, most locks accept emergency power from a 9-volt battery or USB-C on the outside so you can enter a code, and almost all have a physical key cylinder. We set up the key override on every install and show you where the emergency terminals are.

Can smart locks be hacked?

Any connected device can in theory be attacked, but the realistic risks are weak Wi-Fi passwords, shared codes that never get deleted and an old firmware. Choose a lock with encrypted Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, two-factor on the app, and a BHMA Grade 1 or 2 deadbolt behind it. A burglar in Staten Island is far more likely to kick a door with a weak strike plate than to hack a lock, which is why we reinforce the strike on every install.

Which smart lock is best for a NYC apartment?

For rentals, a retrofit lock (August Wi-Fi, SwitchBot Lock Ultra, Level Bolt) that keeps the landlord's key. For co-ops and condos with board rules, a Level Lock+ or Schlage Encode Plus with a conventional look. For houses, a Yale Assure Lock 2 or Schlage Encode Plus keypad deadbolt with Apple Home Key or Google and Alexa support. For a mortise door, a mortise-specific model from Yale, Schlage or igloohome. Tell us the door and we recommend one.

Should I choose a keypad lock or a smart lock?

A keypad-only electronic deadbolt costs less, has no app, nothing to update and nothing to connect, and is often the better choice for an older relative or a rental. A smart lock adds remote unlocking, activity logs, scheduled guest codes and voice control. Many smart locks are also keypad locks, so you can start with codes and connect the app later.

How long does smart lock installation take?

A standard deadbolt swap takes 30-45 minutes including programming. A retrofit lock takes about 20 minutes. A door that needs a new bore, a mortise adapter or strike repair takes 60-90 minutes. Second and third doors in the same visit are faster.

Does the lock still open from the inside without a key?

It must. NYC fire code requires that an apartment door used for exit opens from the inside with one motion and no key, which is why we never install a double-cylinder (key-both-sides) deadbolt on an apartment door and why every smart lock we fit has an inside thumb-turn.

Does a smart lock work with Apple Home, Google Home or Alexa?

Most current models from Yale, Schlage, August, Level and Kwikset work with at least one of them, and several support Apple Home Key so your iPhone or Apple Watch unlocks the door with a tap. Matter-certified locks work across all three. Tell us what you already use and we pick a compatible lock.

Do you service and repair smart locks after installation?

Yes. Battery changes, re-pairing after a new router, adding and deleting codes, firmware updates and realignment after a door swells in summer are all routine calls. Most are solved in one visit.
