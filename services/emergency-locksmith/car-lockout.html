Locked out? Here's what to do

1. Move somewhere safe

If you're on a busy road, walk to a parking lot or side street. Your safety comes first.

2. Call us

Dial (718) 831-6269. Tell us your location and vehicle type. We dispatch the nearest tech.

3. Stay near your car

If it's safe, stay close so our technician can find you. Keep your phone handy.

4. Have ID ready

Driver's license and registration speeds things up. We'll verify and unlock in minutes.

Don't do these things

  • ❌ Don't break a window - it's dangerous and expensive to replace
  • ❌ Don't try to pick the lock yourself - you could damage it
  • ❌ Don't use coat hangers or slim jims - they can trigger airbags
  • ❌ Don't leave kids or pets in the car while you look for help
