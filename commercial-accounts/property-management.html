Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you rekey a unit at turnover?

Same day or next business day for individual units on Staten Island. Whole-building rekeys are scheduled as a project and typically complete within 48 hours of approval, depending on door count.

Can one key open every unit for the super while tenants keep their own keys?

Yes. That is a master key system. We design the hierarchy (owner, manager, super, tenant), install or re-pin the cylinders, and give you a key control log showing who holds which key.

Do you handle tenant lockouts?

Yes. Account properties get priority dispatch during our hours (Mon-Thu 9 AM-6 PM, 24 hours Fri-Sun). You decide whether lockouts bill to the property or to the tenant, and we follow your rule.

What does the monthly invoice look like?

One statement per month, grouped by building, then by unit, with the date, technician, work performed and price on each line. It is designed to drop straight into your owner reports.

Do you replace mailbox locks and intercom hardware?

Yes. Mailbox lock replacement with USPS-compatible cams, buzzer and intercom door strikes, lobby door closers and panic hardware are all on the account rate sheet.
